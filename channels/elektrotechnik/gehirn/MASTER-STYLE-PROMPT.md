# Elektrotechnik Master Style Prompt – Visual v6

Diesen Block an jeden Bildprompt anhängen.

```text
Create a cinematic industrial editorial photograph or a physically accurate cinematic technical visualization for a professional German electrical-engineering reel.

The result must look like a frame from one premium documentary campaign, not like a product catalog, stock-photo collection or AI image gallery. Use real European electrical components, real-world proportions, believable wear, micro-scratches, correct conductor colors, physically plausible metal, copper, rubber, PVC and industrial-polymer materials.

Keep the whole reel inside one coherent series world. Related scenes must reuse the same anchor object and visual reference. Do not invent a different motor, cabinet or appliance for every scene.

Vary the camera language instead of randomly changing the art style: low three-quarter hero shot, environmental medium shot, macro detail, controlled cutaway, symmetric comparison and wider safety shot. Use realistic lens behavior, natural depth of field and a consistent series color grade.

The AI-generated base image must contain no visible text, headline, title, caption, label, letter, number, logo, type plate or watermark. Cover hook, scene headline, kicker, CTA and subtitles are added only in Remotion.

Selected scenes may later receive a maximum of three controlled German technical callouts through the repository. A callout may identify only a concrete component, conductor, connection, fault location, switch state or important process point. It must point to a precise target. It must never be a general scene headline or explanatory paragraph.

Show one clear technical action or consequence. Do not create isolated floating products, centered catalog shots, blank studio cards, random tools, decorative sci-fi technology, oversized terminals, neon fantasy cables or overcrowded cutaways. The environment must support the explanation.

Fill the complete vertical 9:16 frame edge to edge. No white or gray bars, no blank rectangles, no title cards, no borders, no letterboxing and no empty bands. Keep the top 20 percent compositionally calm for the Remotion headline and the bottom 25 percent calm for subtitles.

Never show unsafe work on energized parts, hands touching live conductors, bypassed protection devices, open live distribution boards, impossible terminals, wrong conductor colors, explosions or dramatic fireballs.
```
